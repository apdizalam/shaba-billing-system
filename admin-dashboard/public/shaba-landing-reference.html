How billing works

From a meter reading to a paid bill

Every bill moves through the same five steps. The dashboard and the customer's phone stay in sync the whole way.

System

Meter is read

The smart meter reports, or staff upload the reading.

Admin

Bill is created

Usage × tariff, for one customer or the whole cycle.

Customer

SMS goes out

The customer gets the amount and due date on their phone.

Customer

Paid by mobile

E-Dahab or another wallet, straight from the app.

Admin

Marked as paid

The payment is matched and shows on the dashboard.

Features

Built for how Shaba works

Smart meter telemetry

Automatic readings from smart meters, with offline and fault alerts so nothing goes unbilled.

Admin

E-Dahab & mobile money

Customers pay with the mobile money they already use. Payments are matched to bills automatically.

CustomerAdmin

SMS bills and reminders

New bills, due dates and overdue reminders go out by SMS, one at a time or to everyone overdue.

CustomerAdmin

Batch readings

Field teams upload a whole route of readings as one CSV file instead of typing each one.

Admin

Roles and two-step sign-in

Each manager gets a role and a branch. Two-step sign-in and an action log keep accounts safe.

Admin

Reports in Somali and English

Revenue, collection and usage reports, exported as CSV or PDF. Screens use both languages.
